I am an NLP data scientist at <b>ConcertAI</b>, an organization at the forefront of accelerating oncology research and Real World Data (RWD) curation through cutting-edge AI solutions. My role entails utilizing advanced <b>NLP algorithms</b>, including the omnipresent large language models, to extract valuable real-world evidence (RWE) from <b>healthcare</b> documents of <b>oncology</b> patients.

Before joining ConcertAI, I had the pleasure of working at <b>HSBC Asset Management</b>, where I delved into designing machine learning prototypes and constructing end-to-end data engineering pipelines. This endeavor involved <b>assessing the carbon ratings</b> of market entities by leveraging economic, social, and governance indicators.

I pursued a <b>dual-degree</b> program, successfully earning masters and bachelors degrees in <b>mathematics and scientific computing</b> from the <b>Indian Institute of Technology, Kanpur</b>.

I love programming in <b>Python</b> and I also possess hands-on experience with <b>R & PySpark</b>, as well as proficiency in working within the <b>GCP and AWS</b> ecosystems. Beyond my professional pursuits, I indulge in reading insightful blogs, books, and the Twitter feed to keep my witty bones tingling.
