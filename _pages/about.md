I am an NLP data scientist at **ConcertAI**, an organization at the forefront of accelerating oncology research and Real World Data (RWD) curation through cutting-edge AI solutions. My role entails utilizing advanced **NLP algorithms**, including the omnipresent **large language models**, to extract valuable real-world evidence (RWE) from healthcare documents of **oncology** patients.

Before joining ConcertAI, I had the pleasure of working at **HSBC Asset Management**, where I delved into designing machine learning prototypes and constructing end-to-end data engineering pipelines. This endeavor involved assessing the **carbon ratings** of market entities by leveraging economic, social, and governance indicators.

I pursued a **dual-degree** program, successfully earning masters and bachelors degrees in **mathematics and scientific computing** from the Indian Institute of Technology, Kanpur.

I love programming in **Python** and I also possess hands-on experience with **R & PySpark**, as well as proficiency in working within the **GCP and AWS** ecosystems. Beyond my professional pursuits, I indulge in reading insightful blogs, books, and the Twitter feed to keep my witty bones tingling.
